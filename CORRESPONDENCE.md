# Correspondence: paper ↔ Lean

This document maps the statements of the paper *A fast dynamo with zero topological entropy*
(Lukas Niebel, arXiv:2608.02586v3) to the Lean declarations
that formalise them, so that a reader of the paper can find where each statement is proved and
how closely the Lean statement follows the printed one.  `docs/MODULE_MAP.md` gives the same
correspondence at the level of modules, and the blueprint under `blueprint/` gives it with the
statements written out.

**Conventions.**
- Lean names are given in full, with the `FastDynamo.` namespace; file paths are relative to
  the repository root and name the file in which the declaration is defined.
- The **Source** column gives the paper's printed number and its LaTeX label; unlabelled
  passages are located relative to a label or a section.
- **Status**: `proved` — formalised and proved as stated; `proved*` — proved, with a difference
  from the printed statement or proof that is recorded in `DEVIATIONS.md` (the Notes column gives
  the identifiers of its entries there); `definition` — a definition or predicate rather than a
  theorem (the Notes column gives the entries of `DEVIATIONS.md` where the Lean object differs
  from the paper's); `not formalised` — no Lean counterpart (Part D of `DEVIATIONS.md`).
- The Lean development works in the product model `T² × T` of the three-torus, with the
  velocity in the axially independent class `TransverseRealW1Inf` and `W^{1,∞}` a Fourier graph
  space; the magnetic clauses of Theorem 1.2 are also transported to the literal `Torus 3`, where
  the particle flow, the ideal induction equation and Remark 1.7 live natively.  These standing
  conventions are Part A (A1–A10) of `DEVIATIONS.md` and are not repeated in every row.
- No module contains `sorry` or a project-specific axiom; every audited declaration depends only
  on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` (`FastDynamo/AxiomAudit.lean`).
