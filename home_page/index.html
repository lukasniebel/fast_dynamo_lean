A fast dynamo with zero topological entropy

Lean 4 formalisation of the paper arXiv:2608.02586v3 (Lukas Niebel). Theorem 1.2 is proved in Lean in full, up to the recorded deviations from the paper: its spectral half (the unstable eigenmode and the exact norm law), its growth-rate clause, and its dynamical half (the particle flow, zero topological entropy, the ideal induction group). The blueprint shows, statement by statement, which Lean declarations formalise the paper and how the formal statements and proofs differ from the paper's.
